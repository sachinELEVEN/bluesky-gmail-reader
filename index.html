BlueSky Personal Reader

A private, single-user tool. It lets its owner's personal AI assistant, running on the owner's own computer, access the owner's own Google data read-only, and only when the owner asks:

  • Gmail: search and read the owner's messages (scope gmail.readonly).
  • Google Photos: download only the photos the owner explicitly picks in Google's own photo picker (scope photospicker.mediaitems.readonly).

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It cannot send, delete or change any email or photo.

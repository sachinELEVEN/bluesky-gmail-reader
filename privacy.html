Privacy policy: BlueSky Personal Reader

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

  • Who uses it: only its owner, for their own Google account.
  • What it accesses: Gmail messages using the read-only scope gmail.readonly, and Google Photos items the owner selects in the Google Photos Picker, using photospicker.mediaitems.readonly. It cannot send, delete or modify mail or photos, and it cannot browse the photo library beyond what the owner picks.
  • When: only when the owner asks. There is no background syncing or monitoring.
  • Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own computer to carry out the owner's request (for example, reading an email or sending a picked photo to a contact the owner names). Nothing is sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: access tokens are kept in the owner's local macOS Keychain. Downloaded photos and attachments stay in a local folder on the owner's computer.
  • Revoking access: at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
